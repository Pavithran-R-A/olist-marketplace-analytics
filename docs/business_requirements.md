# Business requirements

## Background

Marketplace leaders need a consistent view of growth, fulfillment, freight, and customer
experience across the available Olist historical data.

## Stakeholders

Operations, Marketplace, Finance, Customer Experience, and Analytics.

## Questions and KPIs

The analysis answers GMV, orders, AOV, status, late delivery, freight burden, review,
regional, seller, category, cohort, and repeat-purchase questions. Definitions are in the
metric dictionary.

## Scope

Historical descriptive analysis, data quality, dimensional modeling, SQL KPI queries,
Python statistical comparison, Power BI semantic specification, and static dashboard.

## Out of scope

Commission revenue, causal attribution, carrier optimization, production forecasting,
customer PII, and transactional system changes.

## Assumptions and acceptance criteria

Orders are the primary business grain. Item totals define GMV. The project is accepted when
source files are profiled, facts reconcile, core KPIs reproduce, tests pass, and five actual
findings support five documented actions.
